# Shielding

Shielding has been reworked in **Ashen Legacy** to reward proactive defense and stronger late-game layering.

Shields now take **0.25 seconds to raise**, meaning effective blocking is about reading attacks and timing your guard before the hit lands. After a successful block, there is a **0.25 second recovery cooldown**, preventing you from chaining blocks indefinitely when you are being overwhelmed.

Blocking is no longer all-or-nothing. When raised, shields **reduce a percentage of incoming damage**, and they now also provide a **passive damage reduction** even when you are not actively blocking. For example, the starting shield grants **65% damage reduction while blocking** and **15% passive damage reduction** at all times.  This also affects shield‑bearing monsters.

Overall, this ensures shields are not an early-game crutch when you are under-geared, while still remaining a meaningful part of your defensive toolkit later through their passive benefit.

## Abilities
Some shields have additional abilities, such as:
- **Corrupted Shield**
	- teleports enemies a short distance away when you successfully block
- **Phantom Shield**
	- provides a dash ability on right-click, allowing you quickly get into the fray and do damage to creatures on the way in, or quickly dash out of trouble

## Enchantments

- **Blocking (I-II):** Provides a 5% increased damage reduction on block per level.
- **Last Resort**: Grants a chance to gain absorption hearts when blocking at low health.
- **Blindness**: Chance to blind attackers on a successful block.
- **Knockback**: Chance to knock back attackers on block.
- **Slowness**: Chance to slow attackers on block.
