# Passive Skill Tree
## Ideas
- Repair Efficiency
	- Can be locked to item types (e.g. swords)
	- Can be locked to enchanted items
	- Can set the durability restored as a % 
		- e.g. +10% dura restored, or even -10% for a negative
- Self Splash Immune
	- See [Clinical Delivery](#clinical-delivery)
- Recipe Unlock
	- Ideas pending.
	- New workbench crafts.
- More Item Bonuses
	- Allows for selection of additional mods on workbench.
- Loot Duplication (multiplier)
	- Archaeology
	- Chests
	- Fishing
	- Gems
	- Mobs
	- Ore
- Lethal Poison
	- Makes your poisons able to kill.
- Item Use Movement Speed
	- e.g. makes it so you are slowed more/less when shielding
- Item Usage Speed
	- e.g. eat foods, potions faster
- Item Durability Loss
	- Make all or certain items lose durability faster/slower.
- Health Reservation
	- e.g. 50% makes it so you lose 50% of your HP, which is unrecoverable.  This can also be reversed, such as if you want a passive skill that negates some other downsides.
- Incoming Healing
	- Boosts/reduces the effectiveness of incoming healing.
- Healing
	- Can provide a chance to recover life (flat or %) on hit, including when certain conditions are met.
- Grant Item
	- Provides item/s once passive is selected.
- Gained Experience
	- Increases/decreases experience gained from fishing, mobs, ore, optionally when conditions are met.
- Gain Experience
	- Gain experience when certain conditions are met (ticking, on kill, damage taken, block, etc.).

## Tree
### Passives
#### Field Repairs
- **Repair Efficiency:** +5% durability restored when repairing **tools & weapons**
#### Scrapper’s Instinct
- **Loot Duplication (Ore):** +3% chance to duplicate **ore drops**
- **Drawback:** tools take **+5% durability loss** while mining
### Notables
#### Artisan's License
- **Recipe Unlock:** new workbench crafts (lead, bell, horse armor, name tag, etc.)
- **Growth from Industry** -10% XP from mobs (growth comes from industry)
#### Toxic Methodology
- **Benefit:** poison you apply **can kill**
- **Drawback:** **-20% direct hit damage** to poisoned targets
#### Bulwark Doctrine
- **Benefit:** On block, apply **Resistance I** for 3 seconds (always)
- **Drawback:** while a shield is equipped, **-10% movement speed** and **-10% item usage speed**
### Keystones

#### Clinical Delivery
* <font color="green">Your splash potions do not affect you.</font>
* <font color="red">Your splash potions do not affect you.</font>
#### Dark Ward
- Gain Dark Ward for 1 minute every 5 minutes.
- Dark Ward: Saves you from lethal damage.
#### Iron Oath
- **Item Durability Loss:** all equipped items lose durability **50% slower**
- **Benefit:** repairing restores **+25% more durability**
- **Drawback:** you gain **-40% XP** from all sources
- Identity: long-run survivalist; slows character progression but makes gear last.
#### The Duplicator’s Tax
- **Loot Duplication:** +20% chance to duplicate loot from **chests, fishing, archaeology**
- **Drawback:** mobs drop **50% less XP** and **-15% movement speed**
- Identity: “loot economy” build; grows via items, not levels.
### Hoardlord’s Covenant
- **Benefit:** Loot duplication +15% for **chests + archaeology + fishing**
- **Drawback:** -50% XP gained (global)
#### Toxic Sovereignty
- **Lethal Poison** + **Healing on Hit**
    - Your poison can kill
    - You have a **15% chance to leech 2% max HP** when hitting poisoned targets
- **Drawback:** **incoming healing reduced by 50%**
- Identity: self-sustaining through aggression, but normal healing is weak.
